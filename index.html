THE AVENGERS

Captain America (Steve Rogers): A super-soldier from World War II who embodies courage, honor, and selflessness. Wielding his iconic vibranium shield, Captain America serves as the moral compass and leader of the Avengers.

Iron Man (Tony Stark): A genius billionaire, inventor, and philanthropist who becomes a superhero using his advanced, high-tech suit of armor. Tony Stark is the brains and wit of the Avengers, bringing innovation and leadership to the team.

Thor Odinson: The Norse God of Thunder from Asgard, wielding the enchanted hammer Mjölnir. Thor is immensely powerful, with a commanding presence and a blend of divine might and humor.

Hulk (Bruce Banner): A brilliant scientist with a tragic flaw—when angered, he transforms into the unstoppable green giant known as the Hulk. He is the team's powerhouse, capable of immense destruction and heroics.

Hawkeye (Clint Barton): A master archer with impeccable aim and a down-to-earth attitude. Armed with a quiver of specialized arrows, Hawkeye brings precision and heart to the Avengers.

Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff): A highly skilled spy, assassin, and martial artist with a mysterious past. Natasha is known for her resourcefulness, intelligence, and unwavering loyalty to her team.

THE X-MEN

Cyclops Scott Summers is a mutant leader with the ability to emit powerful optic blasts from his eyes. As the first member of the X-Men.

Storm Ororo Monroe is the descendant of an ancient line of African priestesses, all of whom have white hair, blue eyes, and the potential to wield magic.

Beast Hank McCoy is a brilliant scientist and a mutant with superhuman strength, agility, and ape-like physical features, including blue fur. Despite his appearance.

Scarlet Witch Wanda Maximoff is a mutant and sorceress with reality-warping abilities. Her immense power and complex emotional struggles make her both a formidable ally and a dangerous adversary.

Rogue Anna Marie is a mutant who can absorb the memories, powers, and abilities of others through touch, Rogue is a powerful and resourceful member of the X-Men.

Wolverine Logan is a mutant with enhanced senses, a powerful healing factor, and retractable claws coated with indestructible adamantium.
